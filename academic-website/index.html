About

Kevin Cope is the Class of 1957 Research Professor of Law at the University of Virginia, with courtesy appointments as Professor of Politics and Professor of Public Policy (Frank Batten School). Substantively, he is interested in political-legal decision-making, immigration, human and civil rights, and judicial ideology.

He is a fellow and past co-president of the Society for Empirical Legal Studies and a co-editor of the inaugural Oxford Handbook of Comparative Immigration Law. He has been a visiting professor at Université Panthéon-Assas (Paris II), the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, and Deakin University (Melbourne). Before joining UVA Law in 2019, he clerked on the Ninth Circuit and two federal trial courts and practiced government enforcement litigation at Skadden, Arps.
